01 · Why Now

Q4 isn't just another quarter for lottery — it's the whole year in one.

Between the run-up to Christmas and the Silvester-Millionen draw on New Year's Eve, German households talk about the lottery more in these three months than at any other point in the year. That's the moment to put Clever Lotto in front of a bigger audience through creators, not just paid media.

Jackpots build, interest builds with them

Eurojackpot and Lotto 6aus49 jackpots tend to climb through the autumn and peak around the holidays, which naturally pulls more first-time and lapsed players back into the category — a much easier moment to convert someone to Clever Lotto than a random Tuesday in March.

Silvester-Millionen is a cultural moment

The New Year's Eve special draw is one of the most talked-about lottery moments in Germany, with millions of extra tickets sold in December alone. It's a ready-made, high-emotion content hook that doesn't need to be manufactured.

Clever Lotto is already the trusted, official option

Next Lotto has spent over a decade building the state-licensed digital rails behind German lottery. Creators don't need to convince anyone lottery is legitimate — they just need to show people the easiest, official way to play it.

That means the message is simple and low-friction from day one: no new product education required.

This proposal is Germany-only, matching Clever Lotto's market. Figures shown for creator tiers and CPMs are estimates from our experience, not commitments — final terms are negotiated creator by creator during scouting, before that creator's content goes live.
02 · Compliance Playbook

Licensed and regulated gives us a head start, not a constraint.

Because Clever Lotto sits on Germany's state-licensed lottery — not an unregulated real-money casino product — it runs under a much lighter compliance load than a typical gambling app. We still plan around German advertising rules from day one so every piece of content is safe to publish and safe for the brand.

✅

State-licensed, mainstream-friendly

As an official lottery product, content can run across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube without the platform-level gambling-ad certification that real-money casino campaigns require.

🔞

Age & responsible-play framing

Every piece of content carries an 18+ note ("Teilnahme ab 18 Jahren"), avoids any framing that suggests lottery as an investment or a way to solve money problems, and never targets or features minors.

🏷️

Baseline on every piece of content

Clear #Werbung/#Anzeige disclosure per German advertising law, no guaranteed-winnings language, and honest framing of odds where relevant.

06 · Guarantee & Terms

The risk sits with us, not with you.

What makes the test safe to run: you don't pre-fund anything, and you only pay for work that actually goes live.

🔥

Performance commitments, negotiated per creator

  • We push to agree a view/performance target with each creator during scouting — it's something we negotiate on every deal, not a blanket guarantee we can promise up front.
  • Where that target is secured and a creator's content doesn't hit it, we reshoot or repost until it does.
  • Final terms depend on the individual creator — we'll flag clearly which creators have a secured target and which don't.
  • Every piece of content is pre-checked against the compliance baseline (18+, disclosure, tone) before it goes live.

How you pay — no upfront risk

  • Invoiced at the end of each month — never before.
  • You only pay for content that has actually been published. Posts that don't go live aren't billed.
